Privacy Policy

Hannah Janawa – Personal Branding Services
Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains what information we collect when you message us on Instagram or Facebook (including through our ads), or book a call with us, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Your name and Instagram or Facebook username, as shared by Meta when you message us.
  • Your answers to our short questions (for example, where your clients come from, your approximate follower count and your budget range).
  • Any messages you send us.
  • If you book a call: your name, email address and, if you choose to give it, your phone number.

How we use it

  • To understand whether our services are a good fit for your business.
  • To reply to you, follow up on your enquiry and schedule calls.
  • To send you information you have asked for about our services.

We do not use your information for any other purpose without asking you first.

Sharing your information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We only share it with tools we use to run our business, such as Meta (Instagram/Facebook messaging), our scheduling tool and our email provider, and only as needed to reply to you and arrange calls.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as needed to respond to your enquiry and, if you become a client, to work with you. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Just send us a message on Instagram or email us at the address below.
